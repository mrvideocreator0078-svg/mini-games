# Neon Breaker: Quantum Impact

**Neon Breaker** is a modernized retro-futuristic synthwave breakout game built from scratch using pure Vanilla Canvas 2D and the Web Audio API.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay & Features
- **20 Handcrafted Quantum Stages:** Complex architectural brick formations ranging from The Gateway to the Quantum Overlord.
- **Dynamic Physics & Angular Deflection:** Paddle curvature determines ball reflection angle; acute edge impacts send balls flying behind reinforced defenses.
- **7 Unique Power-Ups:**
  - **Triple Multi-Ball:** Splits all active balls into three.
  - **Twin Laser Cannons:** Mount blasters to shoot bricks directly.
  - **Plasma Fireball:** Plows through rows of bricks without deflecting.
  - **Bottom Forcefield:** Protective net floor preventing ball loss.
  - **Mega Paddle:** +50% paddle width.
  - **Chrono Slowdown:** Slows balls for pin-point control.
- **Special Brick Ecosystem:**
  - Obsidian Reinforced (multi-hit HP)
  - Explosive TNT Clusters (detonates neighboring bricks)
  - Golden Mystery Bricks (guaranteed power-up drops)
- **Procedural Audio Engine:** Rising synth arpeggios that scale frequencies with unbroken brick combos.
- **Local Persistence:** Unlocked stages and high scores preserved in `localStorage.getItem('neon_breaker_save_v1')`.
