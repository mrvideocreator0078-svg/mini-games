# Astro Miner: Gravity Odyssey

**Astro Miner** is a realistic 2D space simulation combining Newtonian lunar lander flight dynamics with subterranean asteroid crystal mining and hangar upgrades.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay & Features
- **Newtonian Thrust & Gravity Physics:** Realistic mass, inertia, rotational torque, and ion thruster acceleration.
- **Precision Touchdown System:** Landers must maintain descent speed under 110 units and an upright orientation onto designated green pads to refuel and convert mined cargo.
- **Tractor Beam Mining:** Fire a high-energy mining laser at crystal veins embedded in cavern walls (Cyan Beryl, Purple Amethyst, Gold Stellarite). Mined chunks are magnetically drawn into the vessel hold.
- **Hangar Upgrade Shop:** Spend mineral credits 💰 to upgrade Fuel Tank Capacity, Titanium Hull Plating, Ion Thruster Efficiency, and Cargo Hold volume.
- **15 Asteroid Depth Sectors:** Escalating environmental complexity, rocky cavern overhangs, and gravity pockets.
- **Procedural Audio Synthesis:** Low-rumble thruster roar, mining laser frequency oscillation, crystal pickup chimes, and impact alerts.
- **Local Persistence:** Credits and upgrade levels saved in `localStorage.getItem('astro_miner_save_v1')`.
