# Chrono Drift: Outrun 2088

**Chrono Drift** is an authentic 2.5D pseudo-3D retro highway arcade racer built with 100% Vanilla JavaScript, HTML5 Canvas, and procedural Web Audio API sound synthesis.

---

## 🏎️ Gameplay & Features
- **Authentic 2.5D Scanline Road Projection:** Features curvature interpolation, sinusoidal hill elevation shifts, distance fog shaders, and dynamic roadside scenery (cyber palms, roadside totems, retro grids).
- **Player Sports Car Physics:** 3D banking during high-speed drifts, interactive dual-exhaust nitro flame jets, dynamic tire smoke particle generation, and road grip calculations.
- **AI Cyber Traffic:** Multi-lane autonomous traffic with varying speeds, vehicle chassis models, and collision physics.
- **Checkpoint & Turbo Mechanics:** Race against the countdown timer to reach distance gates, activating NOS boost for hyperspeed streaks.
- **Procedural Engine Audio (Web Audio API):** Real-time frequency-modulated sawtooth oscillator engine hum that scales dynamically with vehicle RPM, plus white-noise tire screeching and crash audio.
- **Local Persistence:** High scores, best distances, and total drift credits saved in `localStorage.getItem('chrono_drift_save_v1')`.
