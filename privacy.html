Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Complete disclosures regarding user data, local device storage, and advertising technologies.

Overview

Mini Game Arcade is committed to protecting visitor privacy. This policy explains what information is processed when you visit our website, how third-party advertising technologies function, and how you can exercise control over your personal preferences.

Data We Collect & Local Storage

Mini Game Arcade respects visitor privacy. There is no user registration, no account system, and no personal data is stored on our servers.

The only browser storage directly used by our games is localStorage, which stores your high scores locally on your own device. This score data never leaves your browser.

Advertising and Google AdSense Cookies

This site uses or plans to use Google AdSense and affiliated third-party advertising networks to serve advertisements when you visit our website.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our visitors based on their visits to our site and/or other sites across the Internet.

Opting Out: Visitors may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Third-Party Services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts over HTTPS. When your browser requests a font file, Google may log the request as part of its standard server operations. See the Google Privacy Policy for details.

Contact Form

The contact form on this site composes a message and opens your default email client via a mailto: link. No form data is transmitted to an intermediary database.

Children's Privacy

This site does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. Our arcade games are family-friendly and free from violence.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
