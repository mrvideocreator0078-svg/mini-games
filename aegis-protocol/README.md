# Aegis Protocol: Isometric Cyber Defense

**Aegis Protocol** is a tactical 2.5D isometric tower defense strategy game built with 100% Vanilla JavaScript, Canvas 2D projection rendering, and procedural sound synthesis.

---

## 🛡️ Gameplay & Features
- **2.5D Isometric Grid & Elevation:** Rendered with 3D extruded metallic defense pedestals, cyber roadway waypoints, and animated directional laser turrets.
- **4 Specialized Defense Turrets:**
  - **Gatling Plasma Cannon:** Rapid-fire kinetic projectile defense against light scout droids.
  - **Cryo Beam Emitter:** Chilling cryogenic beam that slows enemy speed by 55% while applying damage over time.
  - **Tesla Coil Pylon:** Chain lightning arc attacking multiple clustered targets simultaneously.
  - **Heavy Railgun Battery:** Long-range armor-piercing kinetic slugs dealing massive impact damage to bosses.
- **Wave Escalation & Boss Mechs:** 20 waves featuring Recon Skimmers, Heavy Cyber Tanks, Phasing Cloaked Drones, and massive Goliath Boss Mechs.
- **Economy & Tactical Upgrades:** Earn cyber credits per neutralization to deploy new turrets and upgrade fire rate and range.
- **Procedural Tactical Sound (Web Audio API):** Laser pulses, electricity discharge sizzles, cryogenic frost sounds, and metallic explosion impacts.
- **Local Persistence:** High scores, waves survived, and total credits saved in `localStorage.getItem('aegis_protocol_save_v1')`.
