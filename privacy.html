Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2024. Short version: we collect nothing and share nothing.

Overview

Mini Game Arcade is committed to your privacy. This policy explains what information (if any) we collect when you visit this site, and how it is used. The short answer is: we collect no personal data, run no analytics, and use no advertising trackers.

Data We Collect

None. This site does not use cookies, does not embed third-party tracking scripts, and does not log your IP address or browsing behaviour. There is no user account system and no database.

The only browser storage used is localStorage, which stores your best game scores locally on your own device. This data never leaves your browser and is not accessible to us.

Third-Party Services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts over HTTPS. When your browser requests a font file, Google may log the request as part of its standard server operations. See the Google Privacy Policy for details. No other third-party services are used.

Contact Form

The contact form on this site composes a message and opens your default email client via a mailto: link. No data is transmitted to our servers — the message is sent directly from your email client. We retain no copy of your message unless you choose to send it.

Children's Privacy

This site does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. Because we collect no data from any visitor, this site is suitable for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please use the contact page.
