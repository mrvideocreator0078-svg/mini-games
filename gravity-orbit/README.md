# Gravity Orbit: Slingshot Odyssey

**Gravity Orbit** is an astrodynamics orbital slingshot physics puzzle game built with 100% Vanilla JavaScript, HTML5 Canvas, and realistic Newtonian N-body gravitational mechanics.

---

## 🚀 Gameplay & Features
- **Newtonian N-Body Gravity Simulation:** Accurate inverse-square gravitational pull ($F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$) applied in real-time across multiple planetary bodies and asteroid fields.
- **2.5D Spherical Radial Shading:** Planets rendered with 3D sphere gradient lighting, illuminated atmosphere rings, and cast gravitational field contour rings.
- **Predictive Trajectory Pathing:** Interactive slingshot aim trajectory line calculating step-by-step orbital arc forecasts prior to probe launch.
- **25 Orbital Sectors:** Solve complex gravity assist vectors to capture scientific orbital beacons, evade rogue asteroids, and dock safely at orbital jump gates.
- **Procedural Doppler Audio (Web Audio API):** Frequency-shifted thruster sweeps, gravity well resonance frequencies, beacon telemetry chimes, and hyperdrive warp booms.
- **Local Persistence:** Level unlock matrix, star ratings, and total orbits logged in `localStorage.getItem('gravity_orbit_save_v1')`.
